## t27b: str and string literals (Closes #6183)

- `str`, `&str`, `string` and `[]const u8` lower to Zig's `[]const u8`: a 16-byte (ptr, len) aggregate passed by address, returned through sret and copied like a struct.
- A string literal is a compile-time value; its bytes (plus a NUL) sit in read-only data, deduplicated, and are written into a `str` place only where one is needed.
- `.len` reads the length (folded for a literal or a string constant); `==` and `!=` compare contents like `std.mem.eql`: two literals fold, anything else calls one synthesized IR function, `__t27b_str_eql`.
